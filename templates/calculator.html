What is a Budget?

A budget is a customized financial plan for a defined period, typically a month or a year. It outlines your projected income and how you plan to spend or save it.

Why is it important?

  • Control & Awareness: Gives you full visibility into where your money goes.
  • Goal Achievement: Helps prioritize savings for emergencies, large purchases, or retirement.
  • Debt Management: Ensures you allocate enough funds to clear off debts systematically.
  • Financial Peace: Reduces anxiety by preventing overspending and living paycheck to paycheck.

3 Popular Budgeting Methods

50/30/20 Budget

Allocates 50% of your income strictly to Needs, 30% to Wants, and 20% to Savings or Debt repayment.

Envelope Budget

Allocates specific cash amounts into physical or digital categories (envelopes). Once empty, no more spending allowed!

Zero-Based Budget

Ensures every single dollar of income is assigned a job (expense, saving, or investment) before taking home pay, leaving $0 unallocated.
